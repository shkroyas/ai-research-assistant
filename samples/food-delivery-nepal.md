# Research brief: Analyze the food delivery market in Nepal

## Summary

Nepal's food delivery market is centered in Kathmandu and is served by a small but growing number of platforms. Foodmandu, founded in 2010, is the pioneer, while Pathao Food and the recently entered Yango are intensifying competition. The market encompasses online meal and grocery ordering delivered by restaurants, retailers, or platforms [F1]. Globally, the food delivery market is growing rapidly, which provides a favorable backdrop for Nepal's nascent digital delivery ecosystem [F10].

## Competitors

- **Foodmandu**: Founded in 2010 and based in Kathmandu, Foodmandu is Nepal's pioneering on-demand food ordering and delivery service. It has reached Series B funding with investors including Team Ventures and True North Associates, and operates across Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan, and Butwal. [F2, F5, F6, F7, F9]
- **Pathao Food**: Pathao is a prominent multi-service platform in Nepal that includes food delivery alongside ride-sharing, and is considered one of the established players competing directly with Foodmandu in the Kathmandu market. [F5, F8, F9]

## Key observations

- Foodmandu is the longest-operating food delivery platform in Nepal, founded in 2010, and is the only one in the Tracxn ranking to have secured Series B funding [F6, F7].
- Pathao Food and Foodmandu are identified as the two established platforms in Nepal's food delivery sector, with Yango recently entering as a new competitor expanding from ride-sharing into food delivery [F9].
- Multiple smaller startups such as Bhoj (founded 2014), BhojMandu (2020), and Foodganj (2020) also operate in Nepal's food delivery space, though most remain unfunded [F7].
- Nepal's online grocery segment is also developing, with at least 33 startups tracked, including Kirana Pasal, Kheti, and Hamro Allpasal [F4].
- Yango's entry into the market signals growing international interest in Nepal's food delivery space and is broadening consumer choices beyond the two incumbent platforms [F9].
- Globally, the food delivery market is projected to grow from approximately $216 billion in 2026 to about $504 billion by 2033 at a CAGR of roughly 12.8%, providing a favorable macro environment for Nepal's market development [F10].
